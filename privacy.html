Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how MODE Property ("we") handles data in YoutubeProject, an internal tool that connects to YouTube through the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the app

YoutubeProject is used only by MODE Property to manage its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app accesses the YouTube account of MODE Property to upload videos and to read or update video details such as titles, descriptions and status. It does not access data of other YouTube users.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and manage MODE Property's own videos.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored on MODE Property's own automation server and are not shared.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or when no longer needed.
